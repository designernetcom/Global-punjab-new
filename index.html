About Us

Established in January 2013 in Magarpatta City, Pune, Global Punjab was created to reflect the warmth of India’s hospitality through a sophisticated, welcoming, and harmonious setting. Our vision was to blend authentic North Indian flavours with world-class service, driven by a passion for serving people and creating memorable dining experiences.
